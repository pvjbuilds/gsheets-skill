Privacy Policy

gsheets · last updated 4 September 2026

gsheets is a personal, open-source command-line tool. It runs entirely on the machine where you install it. It has no server, no backend, and no operator who receives your data.

What it accesses

gsheets requests a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets. This permits reading and writing the contents of Google Sheets spreadsheets. It does not request Google Drive access, so it cannot list, browse, delete, rename, or share files in your account.

It only touches a spreadsheet when you supply that spreadsheet's ID. It has no ability to discover or open sheets you have not explicitly identified.

What is stored, and where

  • After you sign in, Google issues an authorization token. It is written to ~/.config/gspread/ on your own computer, and nowhere else.
  • Spreadsheet contents are read into memory to carry out the operation you asked for. They are not written to any log, cache, or database by this tool.
  • No copy of your token or your spreadsheet data is transmitted to the developer of this tool or to any third party. The only network destination is Google's own Sheets API.

What is not collected

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage statistics, no advertising identifiers. This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to anyone. There is no mechanism in the software by which that could occur.

Retention and deletion

The stored token remains on your machine until you delete it. To remove it:

rm ~/.config/gspread/authorized_user.json

To revoke the application's access to your Google account entirely, visit Google Account → Third-party apps & services and remove it. Revoking access invalidates the stored token immediately.

Your Google data, and Google's terms

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. The full revision history is public in the source repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
